# Cancellation Policy

Appointments can be cancelled or rescheduled free of charge up to 24 hours before the scheduled time. Cancellations can be made by phone, by email, or at the front desk.

Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the appointment, or missed appointments without notice, incur a late cancellation fee of 300 rupees. The fee is added to the patient's next bill.

If a patient misses three appointments without notice within six months, future bookings must be confirmed by phone one day in advance.
