# Insurance

The clinic works with the following insurance partners: CareShield, MediSecure and HealthFirst. Patients covered by these partners can use cashless billing for consultations.

To use insurance, patients must bring their insurance card and a government photo ID to every visit. Coverage is verified at the front desk before the consultation.

For insurers that are not partners, patients pay at the visit and the clinic provides the documents needed to claim a reimbursement directly from the insurer.
