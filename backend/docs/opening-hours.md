# Opening Hours

Sunrise Family Clinic is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM, and on Saturday from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM. The clinic is closed on Sundays and on national public holidays.

Walk-in consultations are accepted on weekdays between 9:00 AM and 12:00 PM, subject to availability. Patients with a booked appointment are always seen first.

The clinic pharmacy counter follows the same hours as the clinic but closes 30 minutes earlier each day.
