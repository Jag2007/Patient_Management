# Billing and Payments

A standard general consultation costs 500 rupees and a specialist consultation costs 900 rupees. Follow-up visits within 7 days of the original consultation are charged at half price.

Patients can pay with cash, debit or credit cards, or UPI. Payment is due at the front desk at the end of each visit, and a printed receipt is always provided.

Itemised bills and copies of past receipts can be requested at the front desk or by email. Requests are processed within 2 working days.
