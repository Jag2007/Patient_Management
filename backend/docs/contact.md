# Contact Information

Sunrise Family Clinic is located at 42 Lakeview Road, Hyderabad. Free parking is available behind the building.

The front desk can be reached by phone at 040-5555-0100 during opening hours, or by email at frontdesk@sunriseclinic.example. Emails are answered within one working day.

For medical emergencies, do not contact the clinic. Call the national emergency number 112 or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.
